# <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">A puma at large</mark>

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">Pumas</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">are</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">large, cat-like <b>animals</b> which are found in America</mark>[^1].

<mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">When <b>reports</b> came into London Zoo that a wild puma had been spotted forty-five miles south of London,</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">they</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">were not taken</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">seriously</mark>.[^2]

<mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">However,</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">as the evidence began to accumulate</mark>, <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">experts from the Zoo</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">felt</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">obliged to investigate</mark>, for <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">the <b>descriptions</b> given by people who claimed to have seen the puma</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">were</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">extraordinarily similar</mark>.[^3]

✦——————✦——————✦——————✦——————✦

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">The <b>hunt</b> for the puma</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">began</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">in a small <b>village</b> where a woman picking blackberries saw ‘a large cat’ only five yards away from her</mark>.[^4]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">It</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">immediately</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">ran away</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">when she saw it</mark>, and <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">experts</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">confirmed</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">that a puma will not attack a human being unless it is cornered</mark>.[^5]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">The search</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">proved</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">difficult</mark>, for <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">the puma</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">was</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">often</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">observed</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">at one place in the morning and at another place twenty miles away in the evening</mark>.[^6]

<mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">Wherever it went</mark>, <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">it</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">left</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">behind it</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">a <b>trail</b> of dead deer and small animals like rabbits</mark>.[^7]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">Paw prints</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">were seen</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">in a number of places</mark> and <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">puma fur</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">was found</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">clinging to bushes</mark>.[^8]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">Several people</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">complained of</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">‘cat-like noises’ at night</mark> and <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">a <b>businessman</b> on a fishing trip</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">saw</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">the puma</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">up a tree</mark>.[^9]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">The experts</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">were</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">now fully <b>convinced</b> that the animal <i>was</i> a puma</mark>, but <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">where</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">had</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">it</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">come</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">from</mark>?[^10]

<mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">As no pumas had been reported missing from any zoo in the country,</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">this one</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">must have been</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">in the <b>possession</b> of a private collector</mark> and <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">somehow</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">managed</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">to escape</mark>.[^11]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">The hunt</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">went on</mark> <mark style="background: #bbdcf2; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">for several weeks</mark>, but <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">the puma</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">was not caught</mark>.[^12]

<mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">It</mark> <mark style="background: #bfe2c9; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">is</mark> <mark style="background: #f4d8c3; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">disturbing</mark> <mark style="background: #edbecd; color: #000; border-radius: 5px; padding: 0px 4px;">to think that a dangerous wild animal is still at large in the quiet countryside</mark>.[^13]

# 📒 注释

[^1]: **SVC** - which 引导的限定关系分句充当**后置修饰语**。
[^2]: **SV** - 时间状语分句中出现了**分隔同位语分句**（reports = that a wild puma had been spotted forty-five miles south of London（**SV**））。
[^3]: **SVC + SVC** - However 是连接副词，充当句子的连接性状语，不是并列连词。连词 for ，将全句切分成了2个分句（见33.2A-1）。
[^4]: **SV** - 介词 in 引导地点状语，介词宾语由复杂名词词组充当。在复杂名词词组中，关系副词 where 引导**限定关系分句**（**SVO**）。在限定关系分句，woman 的后置修饰语是**非限定分句** picking blackberries。
[^5]: **SV + SVO** - 分句②中，that 引导**名词性从属分句**（**SVO**）。
[^6]: **SVC + SV** - 分句②中，状语是由两组“地点状语 + 时间状语”构成的复杂并列状语链。
[^7]: **SVOA** - 由于宾语太长，状语较短，故进行了换位，于是成了 SVAO。
[^8]: **SV + SVC** - 两个分句都是被动语态，换成主动语态后，基本句型就变为了：SVO + SVOC。
[^9]: **SVO + SVOC** - at night 修饰 noises？还是修饰整个分句？
[^10]: **SVC + SVA** - 分句①的形容词词组中，that 引导的名词性从属分句充当形容词 convinced 的**形容词补足语** 。分句②的陈述句语序：it had come from where。
[^11]: **SVA + SVO** - 原因状语分句 SVC。分句①：must have been 表对过去的推测。分句②：非限定分句 to escape 作宾语。
[^12]: **SVA + SV** - 
[^13]: **SVC** - It 作形式主语，不定式分句作真正主语。不定式分句中，名词性 that- 分句作宾语（见32.2A-2）。
