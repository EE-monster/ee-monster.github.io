- `/` = 短停顿（约0.3～0.5秒）
- `//` = 长停顿（约1秒）
- **加粗** = 需要重读

Thank you for giving me this **opportunity** / to introduce myself today. //

My name is Hank. / I was **born and raised** in Dongying, Shandong Province. /
I graduated from Shandong University of Finance and Economics / with a bachelor's degree in **Business Administration**. //

In my previous work experience, / I worked in **graphic design and brand development**. /
During that time, / I was responsible for **market research**, **competitor analysis**, and **brand positioning**. /
I also communicated with clients, / presented solutions based on their needs, / and maintained customer relationships. //

I consider myself a **proactive person**. /
One of my strengths is that I am a **good listener** / and pay attention to details. /
These experiences helped me develop skills in **market analysis**, **communication**, / and **understanding customer needs**, / which I believe are also **important** for foreign trade. //

I am interested in **international business**. /
As China has a strong manufacturing industry, / and many companies are expanding into global markets, /  I believe / foreign trade offers great opportunities for development. //

After careful consideration, / I decided to develop my career in **foreign trade** / and build my **long-term career** here in Dongying. //

Thank you for listening. /
I look forward to discussing more with you.
